/04The network

Bixxville is the network Land of Geb runs on. Every project is decomposed into steps. Each step is granted to a member at a tier, worked, proven with evidence, and verified before it pays.

Value is counted in LOGs and settled on 28-day cycles. Every record carries the network's stamp.
