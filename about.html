About

Our mission is to make complex situations manageable.

Our goal is to bring advancement to human complexity. We do it one operation at a time: find where it overloads, take the overload out, in order.

Land of Geb, LLC is a private network registered in Pennsylvania and headquartered in Christiansted, St. Croix, U.S. Virgin Islands. We run our own work on Bixxville, the system we built to do it.
