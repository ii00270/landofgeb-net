1

The charter

OWAV is the creative and learning body of Land of Geb. It is organized like a university: departments, a faculty, a catalog and a record.

Its members are creatives and artists. They learn, make and show their work together.

OWAV is outdoors oriented. Its work includes real activities outside, not only work at a desk.

Before Land of Geb moved into building, most of its work came through OWAV.

2

3

Outdoors

OWAV is outdoors oriented. A real part of the work is real activity outside.

Outings are planned, led and recorded like any other project. Each one is entered in the record: the date, the place, who came and what was done.

4

Two kinds of work

Small

Assignments

Simple tasks. One person or a pair, a clear brief and a short time. The quickest way to see how we work.

Together

Studios

Joint projects. Several people, one brief, shared credit. The way to see how well we work together.

Every piece of work lists everyone who did it, by name, with their consent.

How a project runs

1

Brief

The work is written down. Each deliverable is done or not done. No price comes before the brief.

2

Review

The project is reviewed and approved before it begins.

3

Work

It follows the same path as every Land of Geb job: assigned, done, proven, checked, paid and recorded.

4

Record

Everyone who did verified work is credited. The entry joins the record.

A notice. University-style describes how OWAV is organized. It is not a legal status. OWAV is not an accredited institution and awards no degrees or credentials. Anything we teach is described on the Services page.
